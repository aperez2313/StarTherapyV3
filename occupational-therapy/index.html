Occupational therapy

Our occupational therapy services support children’s ability to participate more independently in everyday routines, school, and age-appropriate activities, at school, daycare, or our Miami Lakes office.

Areas we work on

What occupational therapy can address.

  • Fine motor development

    Hand strength, grasp, dexterity, and coordination for everyday tasks.

  • Visual motor integration

    Coordinating visual information with motor movements.

  • Handwriting and pre-writing

    Pencil grasp, letter formation, and foundational writing skills.

  • Sensory processing and regulation

    Processing sensory experiences to take part in daily routines.

  • Activities of daily living

    Independence with dressing, grooming, and other self-care.

  • Motor planning and coordination

    Planning and organizing movements, and bilateral coordination.

  • Attention and participation

    Engagement in daily activities, including classroom routines.

  • Feeding skills

    Sensory, motor, and functional feeding difficulties, within the OT scope of practice.

  • School readiness

    Developmental skills that support participation in learning activities.

Services are individualized according to each child’s evaluation results, clinical needs, and therapeutic goals.

It starts with an evaluation

Occupational therapy evaluations may assess fine motor coordination, visual motor integration, sensory processing, motor planning, and functional independence. Findings help determine treatment goals.

Progress you can follow

Therapists monitor progress throughout treatment and adjust goals as clinically appropriate. Parents receive a detailed summary after every session.

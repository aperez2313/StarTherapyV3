Speech-language therapy

At school, daycare, or our Miami Lakes office, our speech-language therapy services support children who experience difficulties with speech production, language development, communication, and social interaction.

Call (305) 778-6144How to get started

Areas we work on

What speech therapy can address.

  • Receptive language

    Understanding spoken language, vocabulary, questions, concepts, and directions.

  • Expressive language

    Vocabulary, grammar, sentence formulation, and expressing thoughts, wants, and needs.

  • Articulation and speech sounds

    Clearer speech and accurate production of speech sounds.

  • Phonological disorders

    Patterns of speech sound errors that interfere with being understood.

  • Social communication

    Conversation, turn-taking, topic maintenance, and interaction across settings.

  • Early language development

    Requesting, labeling, combining words, and early interactions.

  • Fluency

    Support for stuttering and other disruptions in the flow of speech.

  • Augmentative and alternative communication (AAC)

    Communication devices, picture-based systems, gestures, and other methods.

  • Higher-level language

    Comprehension, inferencing, verbal reasoning, and narrative organization.

Services are individualized according to each child’s evaluation results, clinical needs, and therapeutic goals.

It starts with an evaluation

Speech-language evaluations may assess receptive language, expressive language, articulation, phonological skills, social communication, and other areas based on your child’s needs. Results guide individualized treatment recommendations.

Keeping parents involved every step of the way

After every therapy session, parents receive a detailed summary of the activities completed, skills addressed, their child’s progress, and practical strategies to continue working on these skills at home.

Areas we work on

What physical therapy can address.

  • Gross motor skills

    Building skills such as rolling, sitting, crawling, standing and jumping.

  • Balance

    Improving stability during movement, play and everyday activities.

  • Strength

    Building muscle strength to support movement and independence.

  • Coordination

    Helping different parts of the body work together for smoother movements.

  • Walking and mobility

    Supporting walking, stair climbing and moving safely through daily environments.

  • Posture and alignment

    Supporting body positioning during sitting, standing and movement.

  • Motor planning

    Learning how to plan and carry out new movements.

  • Flexibility and range of motion

    Improving joint movement and muscle flexibility for everyday activities.

  • Endurance

    Building stamina for active play and participation in daily routines.

Services are individualized according to each child’s evaluation results, needs and goals.
